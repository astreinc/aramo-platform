## 5. How Aramo Sign works

5.1 **Sending.** You upload a document, add signers and signing fields, and send it. Aramo emails each
signer a secure link. **Signers do not need an Aramo account.** Before signing, each signer agrees to
use electronic records and signatures (the Electronic Signature Consent Disclosure).

5.2 **Evidence.** For each completed document, Aramo creates a completion certificate. It records who
was asked to sign, how each signer was reached, when they viewed and signed, the device information
we collected, and a tamper-evident digital fingerprint (hash) of the document. This helps show what
happened, but **it does not by itself guarantee that a court or other person will accept the
signature** (§6).

5.3 **Reminders and notices.** The Service sends signers emails: requests, reminders, and notices
that a document was completed, declined or voided. These emails show your name as the sender, and
they include a link a signer can use to report a suspicious request (§9).
