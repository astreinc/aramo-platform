## 1. The service and who may use it

Aramo Sign lets an organization prepare documents, send them to a signer for electronic signature,
track progress and keep the completed documents with their signing evidence.

- **Business accounts** are opened by a person authorized to act for the subscribing organization
  ("Subscriber"). The person who opens the account confirms that authority.
- **Personal accounts** are for individual use within the selected plan. Sending from a personal
  account is not available yet.
- The Subscriber is responsible for making sure each document, each signing request and its use of
  electronic signatures is lawful and suitable for the transaction.

## 3. Included usage and acceptable use

- Standard Business and Standard Professional have **no fixed monthly number** of E-Signs, forms or
  templates.
- That unlimited usage is subject to legitimate business purposes, technical security limits and
  reasonable protection against automated abuse, unlawful activity and serious interference with
  the service. Aramo will not apply an undisclosed numerical limit to ordinary use of a plan
  advertised as unlimited; any fair-use action follows these Terms and applicable law.
- **Storage is limited by plan:** 15 MB (Free Personal), 10 GB (Standard Business), 25 GB (Standard
  Professional), by agreement (Enterprise). When storage is full, new uploads are refused; nothing
  already stored is removed.

## 4. Subscriber responsibilities

The Subscriber must keep account information accurate, protect sign-in credentials, send signing
requests only to the intended signer, and not:

- impersonate anyone or obtain a signature through deception;
- send malicious files or content that infringes intellectual-property rights;
- use the service for unlawful or prohibited activity.

## 5. Electronic signatures

Aramo provides the technology to collect electronic signatures and records evidence of each signing
(the signing events, the consent the signer gave, timestamps, and the network address and browser
reported by the signer's device). The Subscriber decides whether a document or transaction may be
signed electronically and whether more identity checks or formalities are needed. Aramo does not
guarantee that every electronic signature meets every jurisdiction's requirements.

## 6. Suspension and cancellation

  period (currently 7 days after a failed payment), or another ground in these Terms.
- Cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid period that applies under the plan's commitment.
  [Annual-commitment and early-termination terms to be approved.]
- Suspension or cancellation never invalidates documents that were already signed, and never
  removes their signing evidence. Envelopes already sent can still be signed.
- Access to documents after termination, export and eventual deletion are governed by the Document
