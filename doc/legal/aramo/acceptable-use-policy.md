## 1. Why this policy exists

Aramo helps people and organizations agree to things and hire people. That only works if the people
who receive an Aramo email or signing request can trust it. This policy sets out what is not
allowed, how we detect and respond to misuse, and how anyone can report it. It applies to anyone who
uses the services, including invited users, API clients and integrations.

## 2. Prohibited: fraud, deception and impersonation

You must not use Aramo to:

2.1 **Phishing and impersonation.**
- Send any request, document or message designed to trick a recipient.
- Pretend to be a company, brand, bank, government body, employer or person you are not. This
  includes imitating Aramo, DocuSign, Microsoft, Google, PayPal, delivery companies or tax
  authorities.
- Use look-alike names, logos or wording to suggest a relationship that does not exist.

2.2 **Credential and data harvesting.** Ask anyone to provide, through a document field or a
link, any of the following unless you are lawfully entitled to them and handle them lawfully:
- passwords or one-time codes;
- bank, card or crypto-wallet details;
- government identification numbers;
- health data;
- other sensitive information.

2.3 **Payment fraud and scams.** Send fake invoices, fake contracts, fake prize, lottery or
inheritance notices, advance-fee schemes, fake job offers or recruitment scams, investment scams,
romance scams or any scheme intended to take money or property dishonestly.

2.4 **Forgery and coercion.** Obtain a signature by forgery, coercion, undue pressure or deception;
sign on someone else's behalf without authority; or alter a document after it has been signed.

## 3. Prohibited: spam and unwanted messages

3.1 **No spam.** Send unsolicited bulk signature requests or messages, or use Aramo to collect or
verify email addresses or phone numbers.

3.2 **No unwanted contact.** Send requests to people who have no reason to expect them, or keep
sending after a recipient has declined or asked you to stop.

3.3 **Recruiting contact.** Contact talent or job seekers through Aramo ATS in breach of the
consent and contactability rules shown in the product, or of applicable anti-spam and
telemarketing laws (for example CAN-SPAM, TCPA, CASL and PECR).

## 4. Prohibited: harmful, unlawful or infringing content

4.1 **Malware and malicious links.** Upload or send malware, malicious links, or files designed to
damage or gain unauthorized access to devices or systems.

4.2 **Unlawful content.** Send content that:
- is unlawful;
- promotes violence or terrorism;
- exploits children (zero tolerance, reported to the authorities);
- facilitates human trafficking;
- is defamatory or harassing;
- discriminates unlawfully, including in hiring.

4.3 **Infringing content.** Upload content that infringes others' intellectual property or privacy
rights.

4.4 **Regulated activity.** Use Aramo for transactions that are illegal where you or the recipient
are, or that require a licence you do not hold.

## 5. Prohibited: misuse of the platform

5.1 **Circumventing limits.**
- Create multiple accounts to get around plan limits or enforcement.
- Share sign-ins.
- Evade verification, robot checks or rate limits.
- Use another person's identity to sign up.

5.2 **Security abuse.**
- Probe, scan or test the vulnerability of the services without our written permission (see
  responsible disclosure, §9).
- Interfere with other users or with the services.
- Access data you are not authorized to see.

5.3 **Automated abuse.**
- Scrape the services.
- Use bots or scripts other than through APIs and features we provide.
- Send automated traffic that degrades the services.

5.4 **Reselling.** Resell, sublicense or provide the services to third parties except as your plan
allows.

## 6. Hiring and employment use (Aramo ATS)

6.1 **Lawful hiring.** You are responsible for using Aramo ATS in compliance with employment,
anti-discrimination, background-check (for example FCRA), immigration and privacy laws that apply to
you.

6.2 **Talent data.** You must not upload or process personal data about talent without a lawful
basis and the notices the law requires. You must respect requests to stop contact or to delete data,
as routed through the product.

## 7. How we detect and respond

7.1 **Detection.** We use automated and human measures to detect misuse. These include:
- robot checks;
- network and sending-pattern signals;
- brand-impersonation and scam-wording checks on document titles and messages;
- link checks;
- recipient reports.

The Privacy Notice describes the data involved.

7.2 **Responses, proportionate to the risk.** They include:
- warnings;
- additional verification (for example a phone code);
- sending limits;
- holding an account for review;
- voiding pending requests;
- pausing sending;
- suspending or closing accounts;
- removing content;
- reporting to the authorities or to affected companies.

7.3 **Proportionate and reviewable.** We use the least restrictive response that addresses the risk.

- **Automated measures** (for example a temporary sending pause) are **temporary**: they lapse unless
  a person confirms them.
- **Lasting actions** (suspension, closure, content removal) are decided by a person.
- Where reasonable and lawful, **we explain the reason**.
- **Anyone affected can ask for a human review**, and give their side, at [support@aramo.ai], or in
  India through the Grievance Officer.
- **Every decision and reinstatement is recorded.**
- We may act immediately when needed to prevent serious harm, and explain afterwards.

7.4 **Business organizations.** When misuse comes from a user in a business organization, we may
notify the organization's administrators. They are responsible for their users' compliance.

## 8. Reporting misuse ("Report this email")

8.1 **Any recipient can report.** Signing emails sent through Aramo Sign include a "Report this
email" link. The report page needs no account. It asks for an optional reason, for example "I don't
know this sender" or "This looks like a scam".

8.2 **What happens.** Reports are reviewed by our team.
- **The weight of a report depends on:** how serious it is, whether it is independent of other
  reports (different recipients and envelopes), and whether it is credible (for example, made from
  the recipient's own signing link).
- **A temporary pause.** Several credible, independent reports may temporarily pause new sending from
  the account while a person reviews it. Existing documents are not affected.
- **Reinstatement and appeal.** A pause can be reversed, and the account holder can appeal.

8.2A **False or malicious reports.** Knowingly making false reports, or coordinating reports to harm
a legitimate sender, is itself misuse of the services. Reports we find to be false carry less weight
in future. We may act against accounts that make them.

8.3 **Other channels.**
- Abuse: [abuse@aramo.ai]
- India Grievance Officer: [name, grievance@aramo.ai]
- Copyright notices: [copyright@aramo.ai]

We do not reveal the reporter's identity to the reported user unless the law requires it.

8.4 **If you received a suspicious request.** Do not enter passwords or payment details. Report it,
and contact the supposed sender through a channel you already trust.

## 9. Responsible disclosure

Security researchers may report vulnerabilities to [security@aramo.ai]. Please give us reasonable
time to fix the issue before disclosure, and do not access, change or delete other users' data.

## 10. Changes

We may update this policy to address new kinds of misuse. Material changes take effect after notice
under the applicable terms. Urgent changes needed to prevent harm may take effect immediately.
