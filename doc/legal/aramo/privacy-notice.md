## 1. Summary

- **Who we are.** We provide Aramo Sign (electronic signatures) and the Aramo platform (applicant
  tracking and organization administration).
- **For businesses using Aramo,** the organization decides what data goes in (documents, talent
  records), and we process it **on its behalf**. Its privacy notice applies to you.
- **For personal accounts and for running our own services** (sign-in, security, abuse prevention,
  billing, the website), **we** decide, and this notice applies.
- **No selling or ad sharing.** We do **not sell** personal information, do **not share** it for
  cross-context behavioral advertising, and do **not** use documents to train artificial-intelligence
  models.
- **Your phone number** is used only to verify you and keep the service safe. Never for marketing.
- **You have rights** to access, correct, delete and export your information, and others depending
  on where you live (§10: India and US states).
- **Where we operate.** Aramo is currently offered only in the **United States and India**.

## 5. Automated checks against scams (how they affect you)

5.1 **What we check.** To protect people from phishing and fraud, we run automated checks:
- **at signup**: robot check, network type, signup patterns, reuse of the same phone or email aliases;
- **when you send**: brand imitation or scam wording in titles and messages, links in documents,
  unusual volume;
- **ongoing**: recipient reports.

5.2 **What the checks can lead to.** Each check adds weighted points. Depending on the total, we may:
- ask you to verify your phone;
- hold your account for a person to review;
- pause sending;
- refuse a signup that shows clear signs of abuse.

5.3 **Your rights over these decisions.** We keep the signals and the reason for each decision. You
can ask for a **human review**, give your view and contest the decision at [support@aramo.ai], or
raise it with our Grievance Officer (India). We do not use these checks to make decisions about anyone's credit,
employment, housing or insurance.

**Your rights as a Data Principal:**

- learn the identities of others we have shared it with;
- **correct, complete, update** or **erase** your data;
- **withdraw consent** at any time; it is as easy to withdraw as it was to give;
- use our **grievance redressal**;
- **nominate** a person to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated.

**How we respond:**
- Contact our **Grievance Officer** (§17). We respond within **[the period in the DPDP Rules;
  target 30 days]**.
- If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the **Data Protection Board of India**.
- If you withdraw consent, we stop processing, except where the law requires us to keep data
  (§9). Withdrawal may mean you can no longer use the service.
- You can ask to receive this notice in **English or any language in the Eighth Schedule** to the
  Constitution of India.

### 10.3 California and other US states

Depending on your state, you have the right to know, access, correct, delete, and receive a portable
copy of your information. You also have the right to opt out of:
- the **sale** of personal information;
- **sharing** for cross-context behavioral advertising;
- **targeted advertising**;
- **profiling** in furtherance of decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

You also have the right to **limit the use of sensitive personal information**.

**We do not sell or share** personal information, do not use it for targeted advertising, and do
not use sensitive personal information for purposes that require a right to limit.

**Categories (California, last 12 months).** We collect:

**How to exercise these rights.**

- **Appeals:** if we decline your request, you can appeal at [privacy@aramo.ai]. If you are not

## 11. Signers (people asked to sign)

- **Our role.** If someone sends you a document through Aramo Sign, we process your name, email
  address, the document, and your signing activity (opens, views, signature, IP address and device
  information) to deliver the request and create the completion certificate.
- **No account needed.** You do not need an Aramo account. We do not use your information for
  marketing, and we do not create a profile of you.
- **Copies and evidence.** The sender, and every other party to the document, receives the completed
  document and certificate. Because those records prove what was agreed, **we cannot delete a
  completed document or its evidence at a signer's request alone** while it is within its retention
  period. You may still ask for access and correction.
- **Suspicious request?** Use "Report this email" in the message, or contact [abuse@aramo.ai].
- **Your rights.** You may contact the sender directly, or us at [privacy@aramo.ai].

No system is perfectly secure. We will notify you and the authorities of a personal-data breach where
the law requires.

and we do not seek verifiable parental consent. We therefore do not knowingly allow anyone under 18
to create an account, and do not knowingly collect children's data. If you believe a child has given us information, contact
[privacy@aramo.ai] and we will delete it.
